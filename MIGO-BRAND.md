# Migo Health — brand guide

Tagline: **A friend in your corner.**

Migo Health is a community health provider that helps people get the care, resources, and ongoing support they need to live healthier lives. "Migo" comes from "amigo." Audience: Medicaid members (English and Spanish speakers) on one side; health plans, providers and community organizations on the other.

Personality: warm, fun, real. A friend in your corner, not an institution. Confident, never corporate.

## Logo

**The mark: "hand on the shoulder."** A thick rounded arc (an arm, Ocean) wraps three quarters of the way around a solid circle (a person, Coral). The arc ends in a small circle (the hand, Yellow) resting at the person's shoulder.

Geometry (100-unit box): arm `M50 14 A36 36 0 1 1 14 50`, stroke 13, round cap; person circle (50,50) r 18; hand circle (14,50) r 10. Small-size cut (32 px and below): stroke 15, person r 19, hand r 11.5.

**The wordmark:** "migohealth" as one word, lowercase, no space. "migo" in Nunito Black (900) in Ink; "health" in Nunito SemiBold (600) in Ocean. Letter spacing -0.02em. Supplied as outlines.

Lockups: horizontal (primary), stacked, with tagline (tagline in Nunito SemiBold 600, left-aligned to the wordmark).

Versions:
- Primary on light (white or Mist).
- Reversed on dark (Ink or Ocean): arm white, hand Yellow, person Coral; "migo" white, "health" Yellow. The mark always shows three colors; it is never two.
- One color: Ocean only, person at 45% tint. White version for Ocean grounds and photos.
- App icon: small-size mark on an Ocean rounded square (radius 7/32).

Clear space: x on every side, where x is the width of the Coral circle. Minimum size: lockup 120 px / 1 in wide; mark 16 px / 0.2 in.

Don't: stretch, rotate, recolor the parts, add shadows or gradients, retype the wordmark in a font, move the mark to the right, place full color on Yellow or Coral.

Ink and Mist were retuned to Ocean’s hue (from #1C2A2A and #F3F5F4) so the neutrals no longer read green next to the blue. Files: `logo/` — outlined SVG masters (no live strokes or fonts) plus transparent PNGs at 2× print-web sizes, app icon (1024, 180), favicon (SVG, 32, 16). Use `migohealth-mark-small.svg` and the favicon below 32 px. `migohealth-logo-primary.svg`, `-reversed`, `-onecolor`, `-onecolor-white`, `-stacked`, `-stacked-reversed`, `migohealth-mark*.svg`, `migohealth-app-icon.svg`.

Proportions: about half white or Mist, then Ocean and Ink. Coral and Yellow together stay under a sixth of any page. Dark grounds use Ink or Ocean. No gradients.

Text pairs (contrast): Ink on white 14.6:1 · Ocean on white 8.7:1 · White on Ocean 8.7:1 · Yellow on Ink 8.4:1 · Ink on Yellow 8.4:1 · Coral on white 2.9:1 (shapes only).

## Typography (Google Fonts)

- **Display:** Nunito. 900 for display, 800 for headlines, 600 for the tagline and subheads. Rounded ends match the wordmark.
- **Body:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (fallback Atkinson Hyperlegible). 400 and 700. Designed for low-vision readers. 18 pt or larger in print.

Scale (slides, 1920×1080): display 80–96 / 1.02, headline 44–56 / 1.1, eyebrow 24 caps with 0.1em tracking, body 26–30 / 1.45.

## Voice

Three adjectives: **Warm** (talk like a neighbor, first names and "we"), **Real** (plain words; say so when something is hard), **Confident** (promise what we can keep, then keep it; no hedging, no hype).

Sample lines:
- "We'll help you find a doctor. We can go with you, too."
- "Confused by your benefits? We've been there. Let's sort it out together."
- "We'll call you next week to see how it went."

Member-facing copy avoids jargon: no "behavioral health," "care coordination," or "social determinants." Say: someone to talk to when things feel heavy; we find doctors, book visits and set up rides; help with food, rent, rides and bills. The formal service names are for plan-facing materials only:
personal outreach and engagement · health education · care coordination · case management · community resources and benefits · ongoing follow-up.

## Icons

Drawn like the mark: Ocean stroke 2.6 on a 24-unit grid, round caps and joins, one solid Coral dot per icon. Each icon sits on a Mist circle and is always paired with a word. Set so far: find a doctor, appointments, rides, food, housing, paperwork.

## Photography

Real members and team, with permission, in everyday places (kitchens, stoops, buses, community rooms). Natural daylight, natural color. People looking at each other, not the camera. No stock models in scrubs, no tablets, no stethoscopes, no crosses or heartbeat lines.

## Applications (as designed)

Business card 3.5×2 in (front white with lockup and name; back Ocean with tagline and the mark cropped off the corner). Social post 1080×1080 (photo on top with a Yellow "Free · Everyone welcome" pill, Ocean headline, lockup and phone in the footer). Community event flyer, US Letter, on white with Ocean headline. Letterhead with lockup top-left at 1.75 in. Lanyard and badge. Plan-facing one-pager header on Ink.
